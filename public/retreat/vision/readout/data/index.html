Three walls

In the second exercise, the team pictured three kinds of people who might come to The Labs and gave each one a wall. For each of our three ways in (Learn: free workshops; Build: twelve weeks on a team; Share: teaching what you know), they wrote sticky notes on three questions: why would this person come, how should it work for them, and what would they get out of it? “Upskiller” is our word for someone on a Build Cycle team.
