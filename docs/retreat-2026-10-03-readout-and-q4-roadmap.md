### The problem spaces

In the afternoon breakouts we named eight tensions in how The Labs runs. They're the starting point for the internal build cycle. If you see a problem that isn't here, you can propose a new one.

| Problem space | What it's about |
| --- | --- |
| Guidance vs. autonomy | How much we steer people through a Build Cycle, and how much we let them find their own way |
| Designed connection vs. organic momentum | Setting up connections between people on purpose, or letting them grow on their own |
| Manual work vs. standing systems | Doing things by hand as they come up, or building systems that keep running |
| Tailored experience vs. scalable model | Shaping the experience to each person, or a model that can grow to new cities |
| Cohort ends vs. community continues | How people stay and keep growing after a twelve-week cycle ends |
| Volunteer goodwill vs. what we ask of it | Recruiting and keeping mentors and moderators without burning them out |
| What we do vs. what we can prove | Showing what changes for people, not just how many we reach |
| Grant dependence vs. diversified revenue | Relying on grants, or building other income |

We've added every How Might We to the field survey in OLOS for this internal cycle, both the ones submitted before the retreat and the ones written after it. Add new ones through the [field survey]([link]).
