Most of the time the right invite is the next workshop. It's free, short, and open to everyone, so it asks very little. For someone already sure they want to build, point them to the next Build Cycle instead.

You don't collect contact information. The site doesn't either. Point people to **theupskillinglabs.org**, where they can Join The Labs themselves. The Deck page always shows the next date and a QR code you can hold up on your phone.

If they say not now, that's a good outcome too. "Totally fair. If it's useful later, it's all on the site." They'll remember that you didn't push.
