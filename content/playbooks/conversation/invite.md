order: 3
title: Invite
readTime: 3
summary: "Offer one specific next step that fits what they told you. For most people, that's the next workshop. Make it easy to say yes and easy to say not yet."
why: "A vague 'you should check it out' rarely turns into action."
shape:
  - "Match the step to the person (see Adapting by audience)."
  - "Name it specifically: what it is, when, where."
  - "Point them to theupskillinglabs.org or show the QR."
  - "Thank them, whatever they say."
examples:
  - "\"The next workshop is Thursday the 14th at the library. It's free and hands-on. Want the link?\""
  - "\"If you'd rather see it first, come to the Kickoff. It's open to anyone, no commitment.\""
  - "\"Here's the site. Everything's on theupskillinglabs.org, including the next date.\""
watchOutFor:
  - "Offering three options at once. Pick the one that fits."
  - "Collecting names or numbers yourself. Send people to the site."
  - "Pushing after a 'not now.' Say thanks and leave the door open."
tryIt: "Look up the next session in the Deck. Practice saying the date, place, and site in one breath."
