title: Adapting by audience
readTime: 3
summary: "The shape stays the same for everyone. What changes is which question you open with and which step you invite them to. Start with the two priority audiences: workshop attendees and people in a career transition."
why: "The right next step depends on who you're talking to."
shape:
  - "Workshop attendee: invite them into a Build Cycle."
  - "Career changer new to tech events: invite them to a workshop first."
  - "Mentor or regional expert: invite them to guide a Pod."
  - "Problem owner: invite them to bring a real problem to a Cycle."
examples:
  - "To a workshop attendee: \"If you liked tonight, the Build Cycle is this, for three months, with a team.\""
  - "To a career changer: \"A lot of people come to their first workshop not sure it's for them. Most stay.\""
  - "To a mentor: \"You wouldn't be teaching. You'd be around when a Pod gets stuck.\""
watchOutFor:
  - "Guessing someone's audience before you've asked anything."
  - "Pitching mentors as if they were participants."
  - "Using 'problem owner' out loud. Say 'someone with a real problem your team could work on.'"
tryIt: "Pick two audiences. For each, write the one-sentence invite you'd use."
