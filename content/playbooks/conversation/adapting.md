The full profiles, with where to find each group, live in Know the Labs under Who we recruit. Here's the short version for conversations.

**Workshop attendees** already like learning with others. Your job is to show them what a Build Cycle adds: a real problem, a team, and something they ship.

**People in a career transition who are new to tech events** often feel like outsiders in the room. Lead with belonging, not skills. The first step is small: one workshop.

**Build Cycle participants** past and present are your best co-recruiters. Ask them to bring one person to the next workshop.

**Mentors and regional experts** want to know the ask is bounded. Be specific about time and what a Pod needs from them.

**Problem owners** are people or organizations living with a real problem. They don't need to be clients, just willing to talk to a team.
