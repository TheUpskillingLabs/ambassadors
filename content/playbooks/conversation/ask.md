order: 1
title: Ask
readTime: 2
summary: "A good 1:1 is Ask, Share, Invite. Start by asking about them, not describing us. You are listening for what they're working toward, what's in the way, and whether they like learning with other people."
why: "People tune in when the conversation is about their own situation."
shape:
  - "Open with an easy, specific question about why they're here."
  - "Follow the thread with one or two 'tell me more' questions."
  - "Listen for a goal, a gap, or a change they're going through."
  - "Play back what you heard in one sentence before you share."
examples:
  - "\"What got you to come out to this tonight?\""
  - "\"What are you hoping to get better at this year?\""
  - "\"It sounds like you're figuring out what comes after your last role. Is that right?\""
watchOutFor:
  - "Asking questions you don't wait for the answer to."
  - "Turning it into an interview. Two or three questions is plenty."
  - "Assuming someone is or isn't 'technical' from how they look or what they do."
tryIt: "Write down three opening questions you could ask at a library workshop. Say each out loud and keep the one that sounds most like you."
