Good Ask questions are easy to answer and hard to answer with one word. "What brings you here?" works almost everywhere. "Are you interested in tech?" usually doesn't, because many of the people we're looking for don't think of themselves that way.

You're listening for three things:

- **A goal.** A new role, a skill, a project they want to start.
- **A gap.** Something they feel is missing: confidence with AI tools, a portfolio, people to build with.
- **A change.** A layoff, a move, a return to work, a career they're outgrowing.

When you hear one of these, say it back. "So you want something you can point to when you're interviewing." That sentence is the bridge into Share.
