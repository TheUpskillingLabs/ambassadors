You'll use this playbook anywhere two people can talk: a library table, a meetup, a hallway after a panel, a coffee. It takes two to five minutes.

The three parts are a shape, not a script. Some conversations spend four minutes on Ask and ten seconds on Invite. That's fine. What matters is that you learn something about them before you tell them about us, and that they leave knowing one thing they could do next.

Each part has its own page. Read them in order the first time, then use the Conversation refresher the night before an event.
