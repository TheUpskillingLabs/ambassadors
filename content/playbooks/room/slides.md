**Present now** opens full screen. Arrow keys, click, or swipe move between slides. Press **F** to toggle full screen.

- **60-second version:** add `?short`.
- **Notes view** on a laptop: add `?notes`. It shows the current slide, the next one, and talk cues.
- **Practice mode:** add `?practice`. Cues show under each slide.

If you want to tailor the deck for a particular event, see **Deck → Customize**. For most events, the boilerplate deck is the right choice.
