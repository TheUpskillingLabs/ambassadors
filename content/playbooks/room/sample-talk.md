*[Example, not a script.]*

**Question.** "Quick show of hands. Who here has changed jobs, or thought about it, in the last couple of years? Keep your hand up if you've tried an AI tool at work and weren't sure you were doing it right. Yeah. Me too."

**Story.** "A year ago I'd just left a job I'd had for eleven years. I knew I needed new skills, but online videos weren't sticking. A friend brought me to a workshop at the library. Three months later I'd built a working tool with four people I'd never met, and I'd presented it to a room like this one."

**What The Labs is.** "That was The Upskilling Labs. It's a free community where people learn by building real projects together. Most people start at a workshop like the ones on this slide. Some go on to a Build Cycle: three months, a small team, a real problem. At the end you show what you built."

**Proof.** "More than 300 people have come through our programs since January."

**Ask.** "The next workshop is Thursday the 14th, 6 pm, right here at the library. It's hands-on, about two hours, and you don't need any experience. Bring a laptop if you have one."

**Join.** "The QR code goes to theupskillinglabs.org, where you can Join The Labs. I'll leave it up. Happy to take questions."
