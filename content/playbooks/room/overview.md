  - "Open with a question for a show of hands."
  - "Tell your 30-second story."
  - "Say what The Labs is in one sentence."
  - "Give one piece of proof."
  - "Make the ask: the next date and what to expect."
  - "Leave the QR up while you take questions."
examples:
  - "\"Quick show of hands: who here has tried an AI tool at work this year?\""
  - "\"I'm Sam. Eighteen months ago I was where a lot of you are.\""
  - "\"The next workshop is Thursday. The link is on the screen and it'll stay up.\""

Room moments come in different sizes: a 60-second slot at the end of a library program, five minutes at a meetup, or a longer spot at a partner event. The shape scales. Every version keeps the opening question, the ask, and the QR.

You can present straight from this site. Open **Deck → Present now** on a laptop or phone. It works offline once you've opened it one time, and the date and QR update themselves.
