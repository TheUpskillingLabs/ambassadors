The most common questions, with approved answers, are in **Know the Labs → Common questions**. The ones people ask in rooms are usually about cost, time, experience, and what happens with the projects.

**On legal status**, be accurate: The Labs is a fiscally sponsored project of Superbloom Design, a registered 501(c)(3). The Labs' own 501(c)(3) application is pending. Don't say The Labs is a 501(c)(3) on its own.

**On politics**, The Labs is nonpartisan. If a question pulls toward a partisan topic, bring it back to what people build and learn.
