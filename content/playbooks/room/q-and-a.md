title: Q&A
readTime: 2
summary: "Keep the QR on screen, repeat each question so the room hears it, and answer in one or two sentences. If you don't know, say so and point them to the site or the coordinator."
why: "Short, honest answers build more trust than complete ones."
shape:
  - "Repeat the question."
  - "Answer in one or two sentences."
  - "Point back to the next step when it fits."
  - "If you don't know: say so, and offer where to find out."
examples:
  - "\"Does it cost anything? No. Workshops and Build Cycles are free.\""
  - "\"How much time is it? A Build Cycle is about three months: six in-person events and a short check-in each week.\""
  - "\"I don't know the answer to that one. It's a good question for the coordinator. Their contact is on the site.\""
watchOutFor:
  - "Guessing about money or partners. Say you'll find out."
  - "Legal status: say 'a fiscally sponsored project of Superbloom Design, a 501(c)(3). Our own status is pending.'"
  - "Taking the QR down to show something else."
tryIt: "Read the Common questions page and pick the three you'd least like to be asked. Practice those."
