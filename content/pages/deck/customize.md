## What you can change

- **Slide 1:** add the host's logo to the bottom rail.
- **Slide 2:** pick a photo from the approved set.
- **Slide 4:** pick a proof point from the approved set.
- **Slide 5:** the date of the next session.

## What to keep as is

- **Slide 1 and slide 3 core text.** These are locked in Canva and marked "Keep as is."
- **Slide 6.** The QR, the URL, and the date stay exactly as they are.
- Fonts, colors, and layout. Everything is Geologica, on the brand's backgrounds.

## 10-minute prep checklist

1. Make a fresh copy of the template. Don't reuse a copy from a past event.
2. Check the "Last updated" date on the template's first page.
3. Add the host logo to slide 1, if they've said yes.
4. Pick a photo for slide 2 and a proof point for slide 4.
5. Set the date on slide 5 to match the schedule on this site.
6. Scan the QR on slide 6 with your phone to check it opens theupskillinglabs.org.
7. Download a PDF as a backup in case the venue has no internet.
