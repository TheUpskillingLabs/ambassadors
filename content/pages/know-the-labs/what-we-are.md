## In one sentence

The Upskilling Labs is a free community where people learn by building real projects together.

## A bit more

The Labs started as a pilot at DC Public Library in fall 2025, with about 50 people. It's built on a simple idea: learning sticks when you use it. People learn, practice, and build together, in person and in the open.

There are two main ways in:

- **Workshops.** Hands-on sessions led by practitioners, open to everyone.
- **Build Cycles.** Three months, a real problem, a small team that ships. Four a year.

AI shows up everywhere, as a tool. It's never the destination.

## What we're not

- Not a school or a bootcamp. There are no grades or tests.
- Not a hiring pipeline. People leave with work they can show and people who've seen what they can do.
- Not partisan. The Labs is nonpartisan, and so is everything we say about it.
