## Why a ladder

Each rung asks a little more and gives a little more. When you invite someone, you're inviting them to the next rung up from where they are. Nobody needs to hear the whole ladder in a first conversation.

## About a Build Cycle

A Build Cycle runs about 12 weeks, in three months:

- **Month one:** dig into a real problem with your Pod.
- **Month two:** decide what to build at the Hackathon.
- **Month three:** build it, test it, and show it.

It takes six in-person events, a five-minute check-in each week (the Learning Log), and the rest on your own time with your team. Everything a team builds is an open-source community project.
