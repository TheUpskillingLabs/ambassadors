## Plain language first

Most people you meet have never heard of a Pod or a Build Cycle. Start with everyday words, "a small team," "a three-month project," and bring in the term once they're interested.

## Say this, not that

| Say | Not |
| --- | --- |
| The Labs | TUL |
| Upskiller, participant, member | student |
| Build Cycle, workshop, session | course, class, lesson, module |
| real problems in your community | civic problems |
| Join The Labs | sign up, get started |
| a fiscally sponsored project of Superbloom Design | a 501(c)(3) |

## Terms

Brand terms are the names of parts of The Labs. Method terms describe how Pods work on problems.
