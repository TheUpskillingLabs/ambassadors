## The minimum

1. **Read Start Here, Know the Labs, and both playbooks once.** About 45 minutes in total, in as many sittings as you like.
2. **Write your 30-second why.** Your Story walks you through it in about ten minutes.
3. **One outreach moment a month.** One real conversation or one short talk. It counts even if nobody says yes.

## Optional extras

- Bring one person with you to a workshop.
- Offer a 60-second slot at an event you already go to.
- Tailor the deck for a partner event (see Deck → Customize).
- Share what's working with the coordinator so we can improve this guide.

## Time

Most ambassadors spend one to three hours a month. If a month is too busy, skip it. Tell the coordinator if you need to step back for longer; there's no penalty.
