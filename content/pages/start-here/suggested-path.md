## Your first week

1. **Know the Labs** (about 15 minutes). What we are, the ladder, who we recruit, and the words we use.
2. **The Conversation** (about 20 minutes). Ask, Share, Invite, plus practice.
3. **Your Story** (about 10 minutes). Write and save your 30-second why.
4. **The Room** (about 25 minutes). The talk shape, a sample talk, and practice with the deck.

## Before every event

- **The night before:** open the Refresher for the mode you'll use. It takes two minutes.
- **At the event:** open Present now if there's a screen. It works offline once you've opened it once.

## When something's unclear

Help & Updates has the coordinator's contact and a log of what's changed in this guide.
