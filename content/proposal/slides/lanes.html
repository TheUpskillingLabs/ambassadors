What I’d count

Six kinds of connection. All of them you can see.

A pod and a local business

They built something. It’s in use. They got asked back.

A person and a job

Somebody got paid for a skill they learned here.

One Lab and another

A playbook borrowed. A tool reused. Someone went and started one.

A Lab and an institution

A library, college or employer keeps coming back.

A town and the wider world

A neighbor reaches a customer far away.

A region and the country

A local lesson changes how the whole network works.

Published every quarter. Against a baseline. Towns with a Lab next to towns without.

Talk track. Same line, six times: the eye should see that these are all the same thing. Each maps onto the Community Capitals Framework (built, financial, human, social, cultural, political), which is the engine of Chapter III. Person-and-job is reported first because it is what participants say they want. Nothing is counted that cannot be observed; the claim is always comparative, never causal.
