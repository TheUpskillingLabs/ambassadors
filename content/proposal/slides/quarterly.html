House rules I’d propose

  • Baseline in issue one. Before we could have moved anything.
  • Compare. Never claim cause.
  • Written like a report to Congress from day one.
  • No adjectives. No advice. The model is the Fed’s Beige Book.

The Labs · A proposal · Keep it close

12

Talk track. The gathering is where we change our own rules, out loud, and write the change into the next issue. The first issue comes from the founding region alone, in year one, and fixes the baseline, the form and the voice; its editor is a founding appointment. The rule-changing function is Ostrom's condition for a durable commons. Under a charter the fourth-quarter issue becomes the statutory report, so it is written in that form from the start. The Beige Book is read on the Hill precisely because it never tells anyone what to think.
