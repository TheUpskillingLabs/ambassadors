The Labs · A proposal · Keep it close

10

Talk track. Open or closed is about who joins; public or private is about who sees the work; the pin follows the work. Project Labs are the Junto's by-products a generation on: a project born in a cycle that runs cycles on its own problems, open to anyone, work public. They can earn pins because the work is on the record, and they are a second route to new Labs in towns where no library has said yes. How one becomes a Lab: first cycle with one of our Poderators beside theirs; second cycle theirs, levels attested by a pin-holder from outside the project; then it stands in the region on the published bar. Guards: credited by name, debrief public, no pay to join, the register shows who hosted. Employer Labs are earned revenue, which the core runs on, and an on-ramp: every employee who finishes a cycle has a rim and knows where the open Lab is. Count the crossings. One hosting agreement, three variants, four lines differ.
