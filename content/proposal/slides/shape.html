Chapter II · How I’d build it

Small groups doing real work. A lean center keeping the standard.

1727

Franklin’s club is the pod.

  • Twelve tradesmen. Every Friday. Real problems.
  • Out of it: a library, a fire company, a hospital, a university.

1844

The YMCA is the skeleton.

  • National body. Local boards, local books.
  • A name you earn. Nobody buys a vote.
  • 180 years without picking a side.

1914

The county agent is the proof.

  • One in nearly every county. Newest knowledge to the people doing the work.
  • Every member of Congress has one. Nobody calls it political.

The Labs · A proposal · Keep it close

08

Talk track. Three precedents for three properties: the Junto for generativity, the Y for durable federated governance, Cooperative Extension (Smith-Lever, 1914) for nonpartisan permanence with a constituency in every district. Borrow the property, not the brand. The warrant is Ashby's law of requisite variety: only a network of autonomous units can match the variety of a thousand workplaces. Archival photographs (Library of Congress, USDA) can replace the years once chosen.
