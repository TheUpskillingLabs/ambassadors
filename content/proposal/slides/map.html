Chapter I · Why here

America’s place in the world is a fact. What its people can do is the only part that moves.

  • The biggest river system on Earth. The biggest farmland. An ocean on each side.
  • Nobody else has that. No election changes it.
  • The one thing that can change in ten years: what people know how to do.
  • That’s what we work on.

Talk track. The picture has no political lines on purpose, the same rule as the expansion film. The geography is the picture, not the argument. The argument: an institution built on a durable fact outlasts one built on a forecast, and among the things that make a country strong, only human capability responds to deliberate effort inside a decade. Everything The Labs does is an intervention on that one term.
