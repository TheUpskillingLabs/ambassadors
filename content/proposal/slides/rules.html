Talk track. Ordered by how early each risk can kill us. Vendor capture and the founder trap are year-two risks; hollowing (Skocpol) and the premature charter are second-decade risks. Library fragility is not listed because it is outside our control; the answer is readiness to host in community colleges, museums and union halls. What the public sees is a community, a pin, a quarterly and a day in Washington. The charter is the one ambition we say out loud.
