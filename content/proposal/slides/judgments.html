Three things I believe

Why here. Why in this order.

01 · The ground

America stays strong.

The map settles it. Build on a fact and the ground never moves under you.

02 · The money

The giving lives here.

Foundations, donors, the habit. We can never run on government money. So we start where the other kind is.

03 · The world

The rest of the world is getting shakier.

Go global before we’re national and the ground moves under every partnership. Get planted here. Then reach.

04

Talk track. The first is the public story; nobody argues with a map. The second and third are internal. Together they turn "America first" from a preference into a derivation: the one place where the foundation is durable, the capital is patient and the order is stable is the one place a fifty-year institution can be founded. Medium powers are hedging out of the post-Cold War arrangements.
