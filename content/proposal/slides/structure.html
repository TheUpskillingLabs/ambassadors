The center

Keeps the standard. Recognizes people. Runs no programs. A perfect cycle should run with nobody from the center in the room.

The region

Runs the programs. Own board, own books, own director. Spins off once it clears a published bar.

The Lab

Tied to a place. Hosted by a library or a college. Where new ideas get tried first.

Simple rules. The people under them can change them. If the center ever goes wrong, take the playbooks and start again.
