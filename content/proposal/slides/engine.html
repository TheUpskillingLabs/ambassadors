Talk track. The line is the research diagram reduced to one stroke: the Community Capitals Framework (Flora and Flora), and Emery and Flora's "Spiraling-Up" (2006): social capital first, political capital last. How we build each: pods, ambassadors and mentors (trust); practice vouched by peers (skills); the archive and the honest debrief (story); earnings, projects, dues, then foundations (money); libraries kept open by being used (places); constituents present, the quarterly, the summit, the charter (weight). One turn: a pod builds for a local business; the business hires and a hire joins the next pod; the artifact is accessioned; the builder is pinned; the team's members attend because these are their constituents; a member cites us unprompted and a foundation gives; the next library says yes; a new metro is new trust, and it turns again.
