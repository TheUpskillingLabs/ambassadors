What it is

  • Named in federal law. Next to the Red Cross, the Scouts, Little League.
  • About ninety groups, ever. No money. No power.
  • An audit and a report to Congress, every year.

The track record

  • None since December 2010.
  • Thirty years the House wouldn’t take them up.
  • The one bill out there has sat since June 2025. Both parties on it.

What it takes

  • Labs everywhere. Ten years of showing up.
  • Clean audits. Both parties. No ask for money.
  • Somebody else introduces the bill.
